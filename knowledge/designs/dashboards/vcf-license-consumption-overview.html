Information on Licensing Policy TextDisplay, HTML

This dashboard provides an overview of total VCF license consumption and its components: vSAN, Automation, and NSX.

VCF and vSAN consumption is measured directly from the platform. To understand the usage of NSX and Automation in your environment, review the NSX and Automation Insights vs. the vSphere Environment Insights.

It is normal to have some virtual machines, networks and hosts not managed by NSX or Automation (legacy workloads, management domains). Use the trend charts to understand the pattern of usage.
