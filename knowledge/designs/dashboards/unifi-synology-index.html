Revised after live recon (2026-09-25, prod)

  • UniFi port, radio and WAN traffic and port error keys are lifetime counters. A rate super metric is not expressible in the DSL, so traffic appears only as labelled cumulative sparklines and errors as labelled lifetime counts.
  • Synology pool and volume capacity keys are wrong in the adapter. Capacity widgets keep their slots, greyed and marked BLOCKED.
  • Synology disk remaining life and iSCSI LUN latency are never populated and are removed. NVMe uncorrectable sectors (-1) show as n/a.
  • Defaults applied: adapter instances keep their native Summary page; the UPS summary ships marked unverified.
