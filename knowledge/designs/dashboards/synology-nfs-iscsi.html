Why this dashboard. The vSphere-facing page: which shares and LUNs back which datastores, how full the shares are against quota, who is mounting them, and how hard vSphere drives each LUN.
Wiring. W1 drives W2, W3, W5, W6. W5 row selection drives W4 and W7. W6 row selection drives W4 and W8. W5 and W6 auto-select their first row, so W4 shows whichever fired last on load (see constraints).
Keys. All keys from `content/sdk-adapters/synology/describe.xml`. Stitch behavior from `content/sdk-adapters/synology/docs/overview.md`. Grey monospace under each value is the exact key the widget binds.
